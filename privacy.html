Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 15, 2026

Al's Basement is an offline single-player macOS game.

Data Collection

Al's Basement does not collect personal data.

The game does not include ads, third-party analytics, tracking SDKs, account login, network multiplayer, or telemetry.

On-Device Data

The game stores progress, high score, controller bindings, and audio settings locally on your Mac using system storage. This information is used only to provide game functionality and is not transmitted by the app.

Network Use

Al's Basement does not require a network connection for gameplay.

Children

Al's Basement is not designed to collect information from children or any other players.
